FREE ONLINE RADIO CANDIDATES FOR TASTE RADIO
Generated 2026-11-05. Every stream below was connected to live on that date.

How big is the pool? The Radio Browser directory (radio-browser.info, which
stations.py will use) lists about 62,600 stations. Plenty of them are news, talk,
sports or religious, but thousands are music. This file has three parts:
  PART 1: my top 50 picks: mostly music, few or no ads, wide variety, and they send song titles
  PART 2: more hand-picked stations, including great ones that DON'T send song titles
  PART 3: a big verified directory: 772 more music stations grouped by genre,
          kept only if they returned a real 'Artist - Title' during the test

'song info: YES' means the stream sends ICY StreamTitle metadata, which
nowplaying.py needs in order to score the current song. Stations without it can still
be listened to, but Taste Radio can't tell what they're playing.
Stream URLs change sometimes. If one dies, search the station name on radio-browser.info.
